About

I'm a product thinker at heart. I love mapping messy workflows, asking the right questions and turning complexity into intuitive experiences that actually make sense to real people.

The best products emerge from curiosity, empathy and continuous iteration. Working in Figma is only one part of my process. The real work happens through conversations, research, systems thinking and understanding how people behave.

For more than 10 years I've designed products across fintech, mobility, food-tech, AI and SaaS, with startups, scale-ups and enterprise teams across Latin America, Europe and the US.

What excites me most is the moment when business goals and human needs finally align.

I design complex digital products and use AI to accelerate research, exploration, prototyping and iteration, without losing the human judgment behind the work.
