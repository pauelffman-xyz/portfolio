Situation & task
A web app made for one corridor, asked to serve more.

SukuPay lets people in the US send money to family in Latin America from the browser, without downloading an app. It started with Guatemala, where it runs co-branded with Zigi and Banco Industrial. Colombia was next, and the web app had Guatemala written into its screens.

The task: one experience that works for any corridor, shows what matters in each one, and makes a send easy to follow from the first tap to the receipt.

My role. I audited the live flow, designed the new home, the navigation, the send flow and its states, built the components in the design system, and wrote the handoff and the user stories for engineering.

Action · 03 Home screen · a modular home

Every market builds its home from the same blocks. Each block switches on or off per corridor, and the badge says what that market can promise. When the promise is speed, speed goes to the front and the rate moves up, smaller. A new corridor is a configuration, not a new home.

Action · 08 Design decisions
What shaped the screens
And what we left out
01
The value prop is a setting, not a headline
Each corridor shows the promise it can keep. Writing "no fees" on a corridor with a fee would cost more trust than it wins.
02
Show what arrives, not only what leaves
Many senders need an exact amount to land: rent, a bill, a set figure. The swap lets them type that number and see the dollars.
03
The navigation hides inside a flow
Four tabs on the main sections, none while sending. A send has one way forward and one way back.
04
Two steps, even with a payment method
Choosing how to pay still reads 1/2 in the header. Paying happens on the review step, next to the summary, so the count never grows to three.
05
Success is for the family, not for the funnel
The receipt is what the family is waiting for. The app download banner went, and a reminder to send again took its place.
06
Built once, skinned per brand
Home box, sender box, header, navigation and summary card are components with tokens. The same pieces carry SukuPay and its co-branded partners.

The handoff
One file, per corridor, every state.

Guatemala and Colombia laid out end to end, from home to receipt, including the edge cases: no balance, balance that covers part of the send, the minimum with Apple Pay or Coinbase. Each flow hangs from a user story in Notion, so engineering builds against the same map.
