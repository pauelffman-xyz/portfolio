Rumbo · a feature inside the belo app
An agent that walks you toward your money goals.

Rumbo reads your movements, your salary, your savings pots and the market. At the right moment it brings you an opportunity with the exact number and the action ready. Nothing moves without your OK.

Situation & task
Eight apps. Money that sits still.

Most people spread their money across about eight finance apps, and mostly just move it from one to another. The information to do something better is all within reach, and still the money sits still.

belo's Agentic Banking Buildathon asked what an agent could do with belo's API. The task I set for us: turn every relevant moment of the month into one clear decision, tied to a goal the person actually has.

The opportunity
Don't add a ninth app. Put the advice inside the one where the money already is.
belo already holds pesos, dollars, stablecoins and crypto. Rumbo reads that, and suggests the next move from one currency to another.

Action · 05 Savings pots with goals
Goals give the agent a direction.

belo already has savings pots. We gave each one a clear goal, an amount and a date, so Rumbo knows what the money is for. On payday it suggests how much to set aside to arrive on time. Before a withdrawal, it shows how far the deadline moves.

  • Payday"Separá USD 160 para Viaje a Brasil": the exact amount to reach the goal by its date.
  • Progress at a glanceA ring per goal, the amount saved against the target, and the date.
  • Before withdrawingHow the withdrawal changes the deadline, before the money leaves.
Action · 06 The moments of Rumbo
When the agent speaks
Only at the right moment, with the number and the action ready
  1. 01PaydaySet aside part of the salary for your goal, based on what's left this month.
  2. 02Buying a tripBuild the goal for that trip: how much, and by when.
  3. 03Idle moneyHow much it loses to inflation, and how much it would earn working in belo.
  4. 04Before a transferSend just what's needed and let the rest keep earning.
  5. 05Before withdrawing from a potHow the withdrawal moves the deadline of your goal.
Action · 07 The traffic light
Investment risk × investor profile
A concept people already know from banks and brokers
Seguro

The risk fits your profile, and your crypto share stays within your ideal

Or the move lowers your risk, like turning idle pesos into dollars.

Moderado

The move takes you over the crypto share of your profile

Or it goes back to pesos, which lose value to inflation.

Riesgoso

The risk is well above your profile

More than 20 points over your ideal crypto share. On a manual move it asks for an extra confirmation instead of blocking: it's your money.

Conservativeup to 5% crypto
Moderateup to 30% crypto
Aggressiveup to 60% crypto

The profile is inferred from history (someone who buys BTC every month reads as moderate) and confirmed by the user. Without history, it comes from three onboarding questions. Every result is a colored dot plus a word, never color alone.

Action · 08 Design decisions
What shaped the screens
Iterated live during the buildathon
01
One recommendation at a time
The first version showed three proposals in a list. In the home it became one card in a bottom sheet: decide or close. The rest live in a carousel inside Rumbo.
02
Every proposal says why
Each card cites the data that triggered it: "The last 3 months you got paid on the 5th and had about ARS 360,000 left unused." Trust comes from the evidence, not the tone.
03
Doing it vs. doing nothing
The detail puts both outcomes side by side, with the same rows. Doing nothing is also a choice, and it has a cost.
04
Risk × profile over an emergency fund
The first traffic light measured months of cushion. We moved to investment risk and investor profile because people already understand it.
05
Look like belo, not like a new app
Inter, 16px radius, grey hairline borders, flat surfaces, thin icons, belo's medium-weight buttons. The only new piece is the night banner, borrowed from belo's own.
06
Copy that sells the outcome
"Que tu plata trabaje por vos" instead of "your AI agent that improves your finances". The benefit first; the AI is the how, not the headline.
Action · 09 Guardrails
What Rumbo does not do
Scenarios for the user to decide, not personal investment advice
No move without an explicit acceptA declined proposal asks why (optional) and doesn't come back the same way.
Freeze in one tapThe agent stops proposing and executing. Money stays where it is, and the user can still move it by hand.
Only what belo's API supportsBuilt on belo's staging REST API. Anything it can't execute appears as a manual step, without an accept button.
Validate the advisory framingRecommending by investor profile is close to personal advice, which is regulated in Argentina. Next step before any launch: belo's legal team.
Result & next
Delivered at the buildathon
A clickable Rumbo inside belo, a two-slide pitch, and a traffic light anyone can read.
Next: test the recommendation sheet and the traffic light copy with real belo users, and measure how many proposals get accepted versus closed, before anything goes near a real balance.
