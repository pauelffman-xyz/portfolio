Situation & task
A system
without a system.

SukuPay's product was growing fast — new partners, new surfaces, new features. But underneath, the design infrastructure hadn't kept up.

The brand green #00C603 — generic, not tech, and failing accessibility — was just the symptom. Below it: hardcoded states, two conflicting semantic systems, variables misaligned between Figma and Tokens Studio, typography never tokenized, and no single source of truth.

The color was the most visible problem. But the real one was structural debt across the entire system.

Action · 02 The color decision
Not one green — a duo
Lime for expression, teal for function
The new green is a pair: lime + teal.

We didn't choose between brand and accessibility — we separated the two jobs. Lime is the expressive accent — vibrant, for the logo, success and rewards. Teal is the functional color: the one that holds every button, accessible across all styles and partners.

Brand · Lime
#61FF61
Expressive accent. Logo, success, celebration. Doesn't carry the burden of being legible everywhere — its contrast is just 1.31:1 on text, and that's fine.
Primary · Teal
#005043
Functional color. Accessible in fill, soft, outline and text. The one a user with low vision can also use without friction. Passes AAA.

The North Star
One system.
From design to code,
no translation.

Completing the migration isn't tokenizing one more color. It's making the entire brand — color, type and dimensions — live in a single chain, and that chain reaching production on its own.

  • A living chain
    Change a variable in Figma and it reaches code. No manual value copying at any step.
  • Every partner is a theme
    Adding a bank is a new skin on the same skeleton — not a redesign from scratch.
  • Devs consume tokens, not hex
    Zero hardcoded values in production. Style Dictionary translates the repo to CSS and code.
  • Everything tokenized
    Color, type and dimensions. Nothing critical lives only as a loose style in Figma.
  • Accessibility by system
    Contrast is guaranteed by the tokens, not by a manual review case by case.
