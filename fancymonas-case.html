🍌 Overview & context
NFTs.
Democratized.

BAG is making its way into the ever-growing world of NFTs, democratizing access to art for artists and the general public alike.

Fancy Monas is the first collection on the platform: 1942 unique works created by Edgardo Giménez, one of Argentina's most recognized visual artists, available as a digital piece and as a physical fine art print.

The design challenge: build the experience of discovering, choosing and buying a work end to end, without crypto and NFT jargon getting between the artist and someone buying digital art for the first time.

🌈 Goal

We rethought the artbag home from scratch, creating a versatile home that can host different kinds of content (editorial, commercial and collection) without losing sight of the number one priority: selling art.

Content 01
Editorial
The artist and the story of the collection up front: who Edgardo Giménez is and why this work matters.
Content 02
Commercial
Direct access to the shop, real-time availability and the price always visible, with no friction on the way to buying.
Content 03
Collection
Favorites, saved works and the "my purchase" status live alongside everything else without competing for attention.
⭐ Home
fancymonas.io
Editorial hero + cart access

The home combines the hero of the featured work ("1942 unique works, created by Edgardo") with a direct CTA to the shop. It always keeps the summary of the purchase in progress at hand: price per work by quantity, volume discounts and the detail of what each piece includes (NFT + fine art print).

Grid · List · Filters

With a collection of 1942 pieces, the shop needed two things: speed to explore and clarity about what is available. We designed two views (a dense grid and a list with a large preview), filters by attribute, and an "Available" toggle that is always visible, so nobody wastes time on works that are already sold.

🐒 Learnings
Learning 01
Design for scale, not for a single piece
With 1942 unique works, every UI pattern (grid, filter, artwork page) had to hold up at the extreme, not just in the demo case.
Learning 02
Lowering the barrier to crypto
Democratizing access to art meant hiding NFT complexity behind a familiar e-commerce flow: price, cart, checkout.
Learning 03
One home, several jobs
Content versatility isn't just aesthetic: it gives BAG one space to launch future collections without redesigning from scratch.
